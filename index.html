Welcome To KeepNotes App...

It is a Note Maker app for study..

Created by Talib Alam.

Welcome To our Note App - a simple, fast, and powerful way to organize your ideas, tasks, and important information, This application is build using IndexedDB, allowing your notes to be stored securely and efficiently in your browser. Create unlimited notes, edit them whenever needed, and delete notes you on longer require. To keep important information easily accessible, you can pin your most important notes and add frequently used notes to your favourite.
